01 / About

About Me

I'm a credit risk analytics leader with deep experience building production-grade ML-powered decisioning programs, monitoring frameworks, and automation pipelines that operate at enterprise scale. My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, statistical modeling, and business strategy.

I've designed and deployed bureau optimization workflows, population stability monitoring systems (PSI/CSI), and end-to-end analytics automation on platforms like Databricks, Snowflake, and PySpark. Whether that's detecting model drift in a credit scoring pipeline or building decisioning architecture for 1M+ monthly credit checks, I bring an engineering-minded approach to every problem.

Published author at Data Science Salon on CSI methodologies for categorical drift monitoring. Research presented at the Midwest Decision Sciences Institute Conference (2022). Vice Chair, IEEE Kansas Communications Society Chapter.

01 / About

About Me

I'm a data analytics professional with deep experience building production-grade monitoring frameworks, credit risk decisioning systems, and automation pipelines that operate at scale. My work sits at the intersection of data engineering, statistical modeling, and business strategy — translating complex signals into decisions that matter.

Over my career I've designed and deployed bureau optimization workflows, population stability monitoring systems (PSI/CSI), and end-to-end analytics automation on platforms like Databricks, Snowflake, and PySpark. I bring a methodical, engineering-minded approach to every problem — whether that's detecting model drift in a credit scoring pipeline or forecasting e-commerce performance for a digital marketing strategy.

I'm a published author at Data Science Salon, where I've written on characteristic stability index methodologies for categorical drift monitoring — a niche but critical problem in production ML systems. I also presented research on e-commerce performance forecasting at the Midwest Decision Sciences Institute Conference (2022).

My approach is simple: build things that work in the real world, document them well, and make them reproducible. If you're looking for someone who can bridge the gap between raw data and operational intelligence — let's talk.
